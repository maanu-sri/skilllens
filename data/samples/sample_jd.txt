Data Analyst Intern

About the role:
You will work with our product and operations teams to turn raw data into decisions. You will present findings to non-technical stakeholders and meet weekly deadlines across multiple projects.

Responsibilities:
- Write SQL queries to extract and clean data from our PostgreSQL database.
- Build dashboards in Power BI and share insights with business teams.
- Automate reports using Python and Pandas.
- Work closely with engineers to debug data quality issues.

Requirements:
- Strong knowledge of SQL and Excel.
- Experience with Python (Pandas, NumPy) for data analysis.
- Good communication skills and the ability to work in a team.
- Basic understanding of statistics and hypothesis testing.

Nice to have:
- Exposure to Tableau or machine learning with scikit-learn.
- Familiarity with Git and version control.
